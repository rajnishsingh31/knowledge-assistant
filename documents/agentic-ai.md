# Here is a concise reference for Agentic AI architecture and concepts:

## Planner: 
Deconstructs complex goals into structured, sequential, or parallel sub-tasks for execution.

## Evaluator: 
Assesses intermediate steps, critiques output quality, and determines if a goal is met.

## Tool Executor: 
Interfaces with external APIs, databases, or systems to perform requested action steps.

## Observation: 
The feedback or data returned from environment interactions after a tool runs.

## Fact Store: 
Structured long-term memory containing verified domain facts, rules, and context.

## Blackboard: 
Shared, central workspace where multi-agent systems read and update collaborative state.

## Manager-Worker: 
Design pattern where a central manager coordinates and delegates sub-tasks to specialized worker agents.
